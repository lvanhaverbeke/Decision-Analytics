The investor is considering acquiring, developing, or renovating a property to provide student accommodation over a long-term investment horizon.

Generate an attractive and financially sustainable investment return.

5.1 Develop the demand function
Use an economically defensible function supported by data or transparent assumptions.
For example, an illustrative exponential demand function is:
Demand(r) = D₀ × EXP[-ε × (r/r₀ − 1)]

r = proposed monthly rent in euros.
r₀ = reference monthly rent in euros.
