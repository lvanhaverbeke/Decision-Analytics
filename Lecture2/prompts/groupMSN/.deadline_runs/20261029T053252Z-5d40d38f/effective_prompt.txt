- Land acquisition and construction costs.
- Operating, maintenance and financing costs.
- Realistic occupancy rates.
- Applicable Brussels housing and rental regulations.
- Long-term investment profitability.

The building should offer three accommodation types:

1. Standard student rooms.
2. Shared studios.
3. Individual studios.
