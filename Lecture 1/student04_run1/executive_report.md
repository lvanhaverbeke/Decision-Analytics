# Student housing near VUB/ULB — executive report

Reference date: 1 October 2026 (year assumed). Research checked 14 October 2026; live sources do not prove their historical content on 1 October.

The proposal is to buy and renovate an existing building for 24 furnished rooms near the VUB/ULB La Plaine corridor. This is a screening design, with no selected property or verified purchase price.

At EUR2,984,600 initial investment and EUR550 monthly rent plus EUR100 utilities, the 20-year model produces NPV EUR-1,239,139 at 6.5% and an IRR of 2.58%. Operating payback: Not reached in 20 years. The assumed acquisition is financially unattractive. The break-even purchase price is approximately EUR698,543, with all other assumptions fixed.

The model includes purchase duty, acquisition costs, renovation, furniture, contingency, operating costs, replacement capex and resale. It excludes borrowing and investor income tax. Occupancy measures paid room-months: 95% requires summer demand. The downside uses ten paid months, lower rent and a construction overrun.

EUR650 monthly housing consumes 50% of the illustrative EUR1300 student budget. Affordability needs direct student evidence and a subsidised room mix. ULB guidance gives a broad EUR500–680 range including charges; its Lucia De Brouckere residence charges EUR491 including EUR61 charges for 2026–27. These are distinct market segments.

Reuse the building, insulate and ventilate well, meter utilities and provide bicycle storage. Illustrative energy consumption falls from 150000 to 72000 kWh/year; this is an engineering assumption, not a PEB certificate. Savings accrue to tenants and are excluded from owner revenue. Survey the structure, flood exposure and heating system before setting retrofit costs.

Planning use, permitted room count, the housing standards applicable from January 2026, fire approval, PEB and compliant leases are mandatory gates. Municipality-specific rules depend on the exact address. No gate is presently verified. Seek conditional due diligence, renegotiate the price and rerun the workbook before making an acquisition decision.
