Student housing in Brussels

Project: Investment for a student housing project, in the VUB.

Prompt: Keeping in mind regulations concerning student housing in Brussels; generate an overview of how this investment will turn out. Include factors like:
- % of international students.
- Minimum wage/income an average VUB student has per year.
- Rent/month.
- Location of construction.
- % of dropouts.

The spreadsheet should present:
- Time needed.
- NPV.
- IRR.
- PBP.

For all of these include type of variable classification.

Also include a visual representation of a graph that shows increase in rent and % of students leaving.
