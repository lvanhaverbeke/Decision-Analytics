# Executive report — Brussels student accommodation

Recommendation: defer capital commitment. The illustrative 100-room scheme, including 20 affordable rooms, has NPV EUR-5,367,708, IRR 3.45%, and simple payback 27.10 years including sale. Discounted payback is not achieved within 31 years. These outcomes depend on project assumptions, not official construction quotations.

The assumed initial spend is EUR11,538,752; required return 7%; opening delay one year; 30 operating years. The default terminal sale uses a 6% capitalization rate and 2% selling cost. Income tax and financing are excluded. The workbook shows operating-only recovery separately and tests zero sale proceeds.

Official survey benchmarks are EUR600 monthly rent including charges and EUR833.50 monthly spending budget for students in student housing ([Perspective survey, pp55/110](https://perspective.brussels/sites/default/files/documents/rapport_enquete_panorama_de_la_vie_etudiante.pdf)). The chosen 30% housing budget share gives an illustrative affordable ceiling of EUR250.05; it is not an official affordability definition. Actual ability to pay requires student income/support data.

Advantages include adding dedicated accommodation, reducing pressure on ordinary housing, providing energy-efficient facilities and offering predictable charges. Drawbacks include uncertain acquisition and construction cost, approvals and delays, turnover and summer vacancies, energy and repair exposure, lower receipts from affordable units and reliance on terminal value.

Shortlist sites near target campuses and public transport. Compare zoning, acquisition price, walking time, safety and competing pipeline. Obtain a site-specific budget, current room prices and vacancies, operator cost plan, tax review and permit/fire/energy confirmations before proceeding. Approve only if updated NPV is nonnegative, IRR meets the hurdle and the affordable commitment is feasible.

Official stock and demand figures do not measure current vacant rooms or a verified shortage; ordinary rental housing also serves students. No verified official project-specific land/build/maintenance prices or investment hurdle were available. The workbook identifies those assumptions explicitly rather than presenting invented statistics. All URLs, periods, formula definitions, sensitivities and cash flows are included in model.xlsx.
